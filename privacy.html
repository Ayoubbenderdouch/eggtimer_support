Egg Timer — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 3, 2026 · Ayoub Benderdouch

Egg Timer ("the app") is built to work without collecting your personal data.

1. No accounts, no servers

Egg Timer needs no account and has no server of its own. Your settings (doneness, egg size, temperature) and timer state are stored only on your device and never leave it. The app works fully offline.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Egg Timer shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as the advertising identifier (IDFA), IP address, coarse location derived from it, and ad-interaction data. On first launch the app asks for consent where required (GDPR/EU via Google's consent framework) and requests App Tracking Transparency permission on iOS. If you decline, you still get ads — just less personalized ones. Details: Google ads policy and AdMob data usage. You can reset or limit the advertising identifier anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking / Apple Advertising.

3. Notifications

The app schedules local notifications on your device so the egg alarm fires on time. These never leave your phone.

4. Live Activities

The countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island is rendered by iOS from data stored on your device only.

5. Data we collect ourselves

None. We collect no names, emails, contacts, photos, precise location, or analytics of our own.

6. Children

The app is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ad content categories like dating and gambling are blocked in our AdMob configuration.

7. Your rights (GDPR/CCPA)

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete. For data processed by Google for advertising, use the consent options in the app (Settings → Ad privacy options where available), the iOS tracking controls above, or Google's ad settings.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version ships with the next app update and is posted at this address.

9. Contact

Questions? Email ayoubbenderdouch@gmail.com.
