What it does

  • Three doneness levels — Soft, Jammy or Hard, shown as real cross-sections.
  • Never miss an egg — the alarm fires on time even if you close the app or lock the phone.
  • Live countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
  • Size & temperature aware — cook times adapt to egg size and fridge-cold eggs.
  • Offline & private — no account, no server, no personal data collected.
